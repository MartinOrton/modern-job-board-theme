Who is behind Modern Job Board?

Modern Job Board is designed, built and supported by 4Mation Digital®, a small, client-focused digital agency in Sandton, Johannesburg. We have been building websites commercially since 1999, for software companies, insurance and underwriting firms, corporate groups and recruitment brands.

That last group is where Modern Job Board comes from. We have built recruitment websites, candidate portals and two-way ATS integrations, and we kept running into the same problems: job board plugins that look ten years old, the features you need spread across a pile of paid add-ons, CVs sitting in public upload folders, and hiring teams emailing job ads to a webmaster. So we built the job board we wanted to hand to our own clients, and then made it available to everyone.

What problems does Modern Job Board solve?

  • No more emailed job ads Employers post, edit and republish their own jobs from a front-end form and dashboard. Nobody needs wp-admin, and nobody waits for the webmaster.
  • CVs that stay private Resumes are kept out of the Media Library and away from your public uploads, and only the people allowed to see them can open them.
  • Jobs that get found Predictive search, clean search URLs and Google for Jobs structured data on every listing come built in.
  • Paid listings without a second system Charge for job posts, job credits or CV access through WooCommerce and the payment gateway you already use.
  • Candidates who come back Job alerts and saved jobs bring people back to your board when a role that fits them is posted.
  • Fits the rest of your stack A REST API, an XML job feed and webhooks connect your board to an ATS, aggregators and automation, and recruiter MCP lets recruiters manage hiring by chatting with an AI assistant such as Claude or Gemini.
