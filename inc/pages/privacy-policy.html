What we collect and why

Please use the demo with test details. The live demo is a shared showcase. Do not upload a real CV or add other people's personal information. Anything you add to the demo may be seen by other demo users, and the demo can be reset at any time.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

What the website stores about your visit

  • Job view counts. When you open a job in the demo, the site stores a one-way hash of your IP address for one hour, so repeat views are not counted twice. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • Abuse protection. The contact form and the job application form keep a count of recent submissions per visitor, for up to one hour, to stop spam. These counts are keyed by a hash, not your IP address.
  • Cookies and browser storage. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run this website, and only as much as they need:

  • Our web hosting provider, which stores the website, its database and server logs.
  • Email delivery. Messages from the website are sent through our hosting provider's mail server or an email delivery service.
  • Google Fonts. The site loads its typeface from Google's servers, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps shows the map in the demo job board, and Google reCAPTCHA can protect forms against spam. When these are switched on, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies.
  • flagcdn.com serves the small country flag images in the demo's currency picker.
  • Payment providers. When you buy a license, payment is handled by the payment provider shown at checkout. We do not see or store your full card details.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

International transfers

Some of these providers, such as Google, process information outside South Africa, including in the United States. When we use them, we rely on their contractual commitments to protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA and the GDPR, as section 72 of POPIA and Chapter V of the GDPR require.

How we protect it

The site runs over HTTPS, passwords are stored as hashes, CVs in the job board are kept outside the public media folder and are only downloadable by signed-in users who are allowed to see them, and access to the site's administration is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so if we become aware of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we hold personal information about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your information, or your demo account;
  • stop using your information, or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it);
  • give you your information in a format you can take elsewhere (GDPR).

To use any of these rights, contact us as set out under Who is responsible. We may need to confirm your identity first. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not happy with how we handled your request, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This website and the demo are not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
