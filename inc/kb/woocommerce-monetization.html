On Pro and Business, Modern Job Board connects to WooCommerce, so that posting a job, featuring it or opening a [mjb_doc lower] can each be a product in your store. Employers pay at your checkout with the gateway you already use, and Modern Job Board takes no commission.

What you can sell

Set it up

  1. Install and set up WooCommerce and a payment gateway. Any gateway that works with WooCommerce works with Modern Job Board, for example PayPal, Stripe, Square, Paystack, Payfast, Yoco or bank transfer. Which ones you can use depends on your country and the provider.
  2. Create a WooCommerce product for each thing you sell, and fill in Modern Job Board's fields on the product's General tab (see Product fields below).
  3. In Settings → Plans & payments → Payments, switch on Require Payment, and enter the product IDs in Submission Product ID (paid posting) and [mjb_doc] Unlock Product ID (single [mjb_doc lower] unlocks). Switch on Paid [mjb_doc] Access to lock [mjb_doc plural lower] until they are bought. A product's ID is shown when you hover over it in Products.
  4. Add [mjb_packages] to a page so recruiters can browse your packages.
  5. Optionally, under Memberships & Trials, give new recruiters trial job credits or trial [mjb_doc lower] access days.

What recruiters see

The Packages tab of the recruiter dashboard shows credits left, published jobs, filled jobs and whether [mjb_doc lower] access is active, with Buy Credits and Browse Packages buttons that lead to your checkout.

  • A pay-per-post job waits as Pending payment until its order is paid.
  • Credits are spent as jobs are posted or featured.
  • Refunded or cancelled orders take their credits or access back.

The admin dashboard reports revenue beside jobs, applications and views.

You can start free and switch on payments later. Existing listings stay as they are.
