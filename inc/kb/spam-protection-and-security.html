Modern Job Board protects its public application and registration forms in several layers. You will find the settings under Settings → People & access.

Built-in protection

  • Hidden field. Every form has a field people cannot see. Bots that fill it in are refused.
  • Time trap. Forms sent faster than a person could fill them in are treated as spam. Set the minimum under Application Security → Form time trap (seconds).
  • Rate limits. Applications and registrations from one visitor are limited per hour.
  • Banned IPs. Add addresses under Application Security → Banned IPs, one per line, to stop them applying or registering.
  • Blocked submissions. Application Security → Blocked submissions lists the last 100 applications and sign-ups that were stopped, with the IP address and the reason. Copy an IP that keeps coming back into Banned IPs.

Google reCAPTCHA

For stronger protection, add reCAPTCHA:

  1. Create a site key and secret key in the Google reCAPTCHA admin console, for your site's domain.
  2. In Settings → People & access → Application Security, switch on Enable reCAPTCHA and paste the Site Key and Secret Key.
  3. Submit a test application to check that it still goes through.

Approving new accounts

Under Registration, switch on Recruiter approval or Candidate approval to hold new accounts as pending until a staff member approves them. This suits boards for a closed group, such as a professional body's members.

Private boards

Settings → Job board → Board Mode → Private job search makes visitors log in before they can browse jobs.

Other safeguards

  • [mjb_doc plural] are kept in a private folder and only sent to signed-in users who are allowed to see them. See [mjb_doc lower] privacy and talent access.
  • Remote feeds on private or local network addresses are refused, so an import cannot be used to reach your internal network.
  • The REST API only shows each recruiter their own jobs and applications, and each candidate their own profile.
