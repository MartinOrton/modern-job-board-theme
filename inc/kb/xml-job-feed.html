Your board publishes an RSS 2.0 feed of its published jobs at:

https://example.com/feed/job-listings

Give this address to job aggregators and partner sites that accept XML feeds. The feed holds the newest jobs, currently up to 100.

What each item contains

Each job includes its title, link and description, plus the company, location and job type in Modern Job Board's own XML fields where they are filled in.

Tips

  • Check the feed in your browser after you switch to the Business plan. If you get a "page not found" error, go to Settings → Permalinks and select Save Changes.
  • A short cache on the feed is fine. See Caching and CDNs.
  • To bring jobs in from someone else's feed, use the import tools instead. See Importing, exporting and backups.
