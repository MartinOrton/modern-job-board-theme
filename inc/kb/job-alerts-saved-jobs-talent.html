Job alerts

Every search can become an alert. Under the filters on the jobs page there is a Get Email Alerts box that repeats the filters the candidate searched with.

  1. The candidate searches the board as usual, down to the jobs they want to hear about.
  2. In the alert box they choose Daily or Weekly from the Email me menu.
  3. They select Create Alert.

From then on, new jobs that match arrive as one digest email, not one email per job. Candidates review, change and delete their alerts on any page with the [mjb_job_alerts] shortcode, so put it on a page they can find, such as their dashboard menu.

Each candidate has a number of free alert slots, set under Settings → Plans & payments → Memberships & Trials → Free job alert slots. On Pro you can sell more slots as WooCommerce products.

Saved jobs

Every job page has a Save button beside Apply. Saved jobs are listed under Saved jobs in the candidate dashboard, so candidates need an account to save a job and can find it again on any device.

The talent pool

The talent pool lets recruiters browse candidates. Add it to a page with [mjb_talent_pool].

  • Candidates are only listed once they switch on recruiter visibility in their dashboard. It is off by default.
  • Settings → People & access → CV Privacy & Talent decides who may browse the pool. See CV privacy and talent access.
