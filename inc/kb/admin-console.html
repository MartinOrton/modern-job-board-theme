Everything for the board lives under Modern Job Board in the wp-admin menu. The screens are tabs inside one console, and each tab loads without a full page reload. You can also link straight to a tab: admin.php?page=modern-job-board&tab=jobs.

The tabs

The dashboard

The dashboard is the place to start each day.

  • Board health checks your set-up: missing pages, structured data that is not active, background tasks that aren't scheduled, and search engines being blocked from the site. Each warning links to the fix.
  • Open items counts what is waiting for you, such as jobs pending review and new applications.
  • Latest jobs lists the newest listings with their status.
  • Plan and capacity shows your plan and, on Free, how many of your 100 published jobs you are using.

Import Jobs and Export Jobs buttons at the top lead to the Tools tab.

The jobs console

The Jobs tab is built for boards with hundreds of listings.

  • Views split the list into All, Needs attention, Expiring, Zero views, No applications, Published, Pending, Draft, Expired and Filled. The number beside each view tells you how many jobs it holds.
  • Search suggests jobs, companies and locations as you type. Choose a suggestion or press Enter to filter the list; typing alone does not reload the table.
  • Filters and sorting narrow the list further. Your choices of density and rows per page are remembered in your browser.
  • Recruiter shows who posted each job, with Admin, Recruiter or Board staff under the name. Select a name to show only that person's jobs, or use the Recruiter filter. On a company career portal the column is called Posted by and starts hidden.
  • Columns shows or hides Recruiter, Applications, Views, Posted and Expires. Your choice is remembered for your account.
  • Bulk actions apply one change to all the jobs you tick.
  • Export downloads the current list as a CSV file, including who posted each job.

Start with the Needs attention view. The warning icon beside it means some jobs are waiting for you.
