Where [mjb_doc plural lower] are stored

Uploaded [mjb_doc plural lower] are written to wp-content/mjb-private/, outside the Media Library, under names that cannot be guessed. They never get a public link. Logos and profile photos go to wp-content/mjb-brand/, which is public by design.

Your web server must refuse direct requests to wp-content/mjb-private/. Apache and LiteSpeed do this automatically; nginx needs one rule. Check it with the steps in File storage and server setup.

What happens when someone opens a [mjb_doc lower]

  1. A recruiter opens an application in their dashboard and selects the [mjb_doc lower].
  2. Modern Job Board checks that they are signed in, that the link's security token is valid, and that they are allowed to see that application.
  3. The file is read from the private folder for that one request. No public address is created.

A copied [mjb_doc lower] link does nothing for anyone who is not signed in and allowed to see it.

Who can browse candidates

By default, recruiters can open the [mjb_doc plural lower] of candidates who applied to their own jobs (On application in Settings → People & access → [mjb_doc] Privacy & Talent). Who may browse everyone else in the talent pool is set by Grant [mjb_doc lower] / talent access:

Candidates only appear in the talent pool if they switch on recruiter visibility in their profile. It is off by default.

The candidate anonymizer

The Candidate anonymizer hides surnames, so a candidate appears as "Jane D." to recruiters browsing the pool. You can also ask search engines not to index public talent profiles.

POPIA and GDPR

These tools help you protect candidates' personal information: private storage, checked downloads, access levels, opt-in talent profiles and the anonymizer. Whether your board complies with the law still depends on how you collect, use and keep personal data, and on your own privacy policy.
