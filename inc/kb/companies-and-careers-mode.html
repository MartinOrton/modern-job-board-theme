Companies

Every recruiter's company is a public company page. The directory at /jobs/companies/ lists each company that has at least one published job, and each company page lists that employer's open jobs.

Recruiters fill in their company on the Company tab of their dashboard: name, tagline, description, website, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and a logo. On job cards, hovering over the company name shows a preview with the logo (or the company's initials when there is no logo) and the links the company has filled in.

You can edit any company under Modern Job Board → Companies, and showcase chosen companies anywhere with [mjb_featured_companies].

Careers mode

If the site lists jobs for one employer only, such as your own company's careers page, switch the board to careers mode in Settings → Job board → Board Mode → Board type.

In careers mode:

  • Candidates can still browse, apply, save jobs and set up alerts.
  • The front-end job form, recruiter dashboard, talent pool, packages and featured companies are switched off for visitors, even if their shortcodes are on a page. The Post a Job link sends visitors to the jobs list.
  • Settings for paid features are hidden.
  • Recruiter login and registration stay available for your hiring team, and the company directory and company pages stay available.

You post and manage jobs in wp-admin. Switching back to a job board restores your earlier paid-feature settings.
