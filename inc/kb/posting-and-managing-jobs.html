How a job gets posted

  1. A recruiter opens the job form (the Job Submission Form block, usually on a Post a Job page) and fills in the job: title, description, company, location, type, category and how to apply. Custom fields you have added appear on the form too (Pro).
  2. They submit it. The job is saved as Pending for your review.
  3. You review it under Modern Job Board → Jobs (the Pending view) and publish it, or edit it first.

With Auto-approve trusted recruiters on (the default), a recruiter who already has a published job skips the review: their new jobs go live at once. Their first job is always reviewed. Jobs saved as drafts, or edited in wp-admin, are never published automatically.

On a paid board (Pro), a job that needs payment waits as Pending payment until its WooCommerce order is paid, then goes live. See Charging employers with WooCommerce.

Who can post

Settings → Job board → Job Posting Rules → Who can post jobs decides who sees the form: recruiters only (the default), administrators only, or anyone with an account.

Expiry

Each job runs for the number of days in Settings → Listings → Listing Duration. A daily check changes jobs past their end date to Expired, which removes them from the public list. Expired jobs stay in the recruiter's dashboard and in wp-admin.

Managing jobs from the recruiter dashboard

On the Jobs tab of their dashboard, recruiters can:

  • Edit a job, which reopens it in the job form.
  • Mark filled when the role is taken. A filled job shows as filled, or disappears from the list if Hide filled jobs is on.
  • Reopen a filled job.
  • Republish a draft or expired job. This clears the filled flag and gives the job a new end date.
  • Feature this job to pin it with a Featured badge. On a paid board this uses a credit or goes through checkout (Pro).
  • Delete a job, after a warning. It moves to a Deleted Jobs list under their jobs, where they can Restore it to the status it had for 30 days (your WordPress trash period).

Managing jobs in wp-admin

The jobs console lists every job with views for Pending, Expiring, Expired, Filled and more, plus bulk actions and CSV export. See The admin console.

On the Free plan, publishing is limited to 100 jobs at a time. Over the limit, a new job stays Pending. See Plans and license keys.
