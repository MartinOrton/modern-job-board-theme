What is added for you

Every job page carries JobPosting structured data, written from the listing: title, description, date posted, employer, location, employment type and a direct-apply flag. Search engines read it to show jobs in dedicated results such as Google for Jobs.

The board also gives each job a readable URL built from its location and title, links each job to related jobs, and gives category, location and company pages clearer titles and meta descriptions.

Map your job types

Google expects a fixed set of employment types, such as FULL_TIME, PART_TIME and CONTRACTOR. Your own job types may be called something else.

  1. Open Settings → Integrations → Google Jobs & Webhooks.
  2. In the Google Jobs type map, choose the Google type for each of your job types. Types you do not map are matched by name.
  3. Under Google Jobs static fields, you can set a base salary currency, value and unit for every job, if that applies to your board.

Check the markup

  1. Open a published job and copy its address.
  2. Paste it into Google's Rich Results Test. It should find a Job posting with no errors.
  3. Fix any warnings in the job itself: salary and a full location help most.

The admin dashboard's Board health warns you if search engines are discouraged from indexing the site (Settings → Reading in WordPress). Staging sites should block indexing; live sites must not.

Structured data makes a job eligible for Google for Jobs, but Google decides what it shows. Keep jobs current, fill in salary and location, and mark roles filled so your listings stay trustworthy.
