The emails the board sends

Job alert digests are sent on the schedule each candidate chose. See Job alerts, saved jobs and the talent pool.

Change the wording

Open Modern Job Board → Emails and choose an email. Edit its subject and body, then save. Words in curly brackets are filled in when the email is sent:

Brand the emails

Under Settings → Integrations → Email, switch on Branded emails to send every message in an HTML layout with your Logo, accent Colour and Footer text. The footer can use {site_link}, your site name linked to your site. Powered by Modern Job Board is added below the footer. Both footer links carry Google Analytics (UTM) tags: links to your site use source notification, medium email and the type of email as the campaign, so you can see in Analytics which emails bring people back. Recruiter versions lets recruiters adjust the wording of the candidate emails they send.

Make sure emails arrive

By default WordPress sends mail through your server's PHP mail function. Many hosts deliver this badly, or not at all, so for a live board send through a proper mail server:

  1. In Settings → Integrations → Email, switch on Send through SMTP.
  2. Enter your mail provider's SMTP host, Port and Encryption (usually port 587 with TLS, or 465 with SSL).
  3. Switch on Authentication and enter the Username and Password your provider gave you.
  4. Set the From email to an address on your own domain, and a From name such as your board's name.
  5. Save, then use Test email to send yourself a message.

Use a From address on your own domain, and set up SPF and DKIM for it with your mail provider. Without them, many inboxes send board emails to spam.

To check your setup, open Tools → Health. Email Delivery shows how the board sends email, the sender address and the sender name, and marks anything that tends to send email to spam: no SMTP, WordPress's default wordpress@ address (which has no mailbox behind it), or a sender on a different domain from your site. The Dashboard's Board health list shows the same check. When no From name is set, emails use your site name.

Mailchimp

Switch on Enable Mailchimp, paste your Mailchimp API key, and enter the Recruiter audience ID and Candidate audience ID. New recruiters and candidates who agree to receive news are added to the matching audience.

Candidates control their own email

In the candidate dashboard, under Account settings, candidates can switch off application emails and recruiter-message emails. Tips and news stay off until a candidate turns them on.
