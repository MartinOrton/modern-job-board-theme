The Custom Fields Builder adds your own fields to the job form or the application form. Values are saved with the job or application, shown beside it, and returned with each job by the REST API.

Add a field

  1. Open Modern Job Board → Custom Fields.
  2. Add a field and give it a label.
  3. Choose its type (see below).
  4. Choose where it appears: on the job form, or on the application form.
  5. Save. The field appears on the form straight away.

Field types

Job fields appear on the job submission form and on the job page. Application fields appear on the form candidates fill in when they apply, and recruiters see the answers with each application.

Keep application forms short. Every extra required field means fewer candidates finish applying.
