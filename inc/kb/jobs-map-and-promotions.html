The jobs map

The jobs map plots every open job as a marker on a Google Map.

  1. Create a Google Maps API key in the Google Cloud console, with the Maps JavaScript API enabled. Restrict the key to your site's domain.
  2. Paste it into Settings → Job board → Listings → Google Maps API Key.
  3. Add [mjb_jobs_map] to a page.
  4. Set Jobs map height and Jobs map max markers to suit the page.

Each job page can also show a small map of its own location. Without a key, maps show a reminder to add one instead of a broken map.

Google may charge for map loads beyond its monthly free allowance. Set a budget alert in the Google Cloud console.

Featured jobs

A featured job gets a Featured badge and a highlighted card in the job list. You can feature a job in wp-admin, and recruiters can feature their own jobs with Feature this job in their dashboard. On a paid board (Pro) featuring costs a credit or goes through checkout.

Featured companies

Show a set of employers you choose with [mjb_featured_companies], for example on your home page.

Ad banners

Create banners in Settings → Plans & payments → Ad Banners and choose where they appear: position 0 puts a banner above the job list, 5 puts it after the fifth job card. To show a banner anywhere else, place [mjb_ad_banner position="0"] or [mjb_ad_banner label="Name"] on the page. Banner and featured spots are yours to sell to sponsors directly.

Sharing and social posting

  • Every job page ends with Apply, Save and Share. Share opens LinkedIn, X, Facebook, email and a copy-link button.
  • A job can take applications over WhatsApp instead of the on-site form.
  • Settings → Integrations → Social Auto-Post sends each newly published job as JSON to a webhook address. Connect it to Zapier, Make or n8n to post new jobs to your social channels automatically.
