What each plan unlocks

Prices and the full comparison are on the pricing section. Licensing terms, renewals and refunds are explained in the License & Refund Policy.

Activate a license key

  1. Buy a plan. Your license key is sent to you by email after payment.
  2. In wp-admin, go to Modern Job Board → Settings, open Plans & payments → License & Plan, and paste the key into License key.
  3. Select Save Changes. Current plan shows the plan the key unlocked, and the paid features are available straight away.

Keys look like MJB-PRO-20271119-ABCD1234: the plan, the date the license runs to, and a checksum. Activation works offline; the plugin does not need to contact our servers.

When a license reaches its end date, the paid features you activated keep working. You stop receiving updates and support until you renew.

Free plan limits

On the Free plan, up to 100 jobs can be published at the same time. Only published jobs count: drafts, pending and expired jobs do not, but a filled job that is still published does. When the limit is reached, a newly submitted job is held as Pending instead of going live, and the admin shows a notice. Editing a job that is already published never counts as a new one.

Set a plan in wp-config.php

On a staging or development copy of your site you can force a plan without a key:

define( 'MJB_LICENSE_PLAN', 'business' ); // free, pro, business or complete_site

Add the line to wp-config.php above the line that says "That's all, stop editing!". Use this only on sites you own and for the plan you have bought.
