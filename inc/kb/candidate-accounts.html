Candidate signup

The Candidate Registration block ([mjb_candidate_registration]) takes candidates through three steps:

  1. About you: their name and contact details. Phone numbers are stored in international form.
  2. Your CV: a CV file is required. A profile photo is optional and is cropped to a square when both sides are at least 200 pixels.
  3. Your account: the email address and password they sign in with.

If Candidate approval is on (Settings → People & access → Registration), the account stays pending until a staff member approves it, and the candidate is told by email.

The candidate dashboard

Applying for a job

Candidates apply from the job page with a message and the CV of their choice. Each application keeps its own copy of the CV, so replacing a CV later does not change what a recruiter already received.

As the recruiter works through the application, its stage changes: New, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Rejected or Hired. The candidate sees the stage in their dashboard and can be emailed when it changes.

Email choices

Under Account settings, candidates can switch off application emails and recruiter-message emails. Tips and news stay off until they turn them on.

Messages

When the [mjb_messages] shortcode is on a page, candidates and recruiters can message each other inside the board.
