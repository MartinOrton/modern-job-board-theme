Page caching makes a job board faster, but some pages show different content to each person and must never be cached.

Exclusions to add

Most caching plugins have a setting for pages or URLs that are never cached. Add the pages that hold these blocks, by their address on your site:

  • Recruiter Dashboard and Candidate Dashboard
  • Recruiter Login, Candidate Login, Recruiter Registration, Candidate Registration
  • Job Submission Form
  • WooCommerce cart, checkout and account pages, if you use paid posting

And the URL fragment mjb_download.

After a major plugin update, clear your page cache so visitors get the new styles and scripts.
