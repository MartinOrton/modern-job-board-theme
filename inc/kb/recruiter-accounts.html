Recruiter signup

The Recruiter Registration block ([mjb_employer_registration]) has three steps:

  1. You: the recruiter's name and contact details.
  2. Company: the company name (required) and logo (optional).
  3. Account: email and password.

If Recruiter approval is on (Settings → People & access → Registration), new recruiter accounts stay pending until a staff member approves them.

The recruiter dashboard

Working with applications

Each application has a stage: New, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Rejected or Hired. Recruiters change the stage from the Applications tab, and the candidate can be emailed each time.

From an application, recruiters can open the candidate's CV and send one of the ready-made emails: Interview Invitation, Application Declined or Application Status, with a personal note. Your site's wording for these emails is edited under Modern Job Board → Emails. When Recruiter versions is on (Settings → Integrations → Email), recruiters can write their own versions.

An Outreach Draft template helps recruiters write to a candidate from their own email. The board never sends it.

What recruiters can see

Recruiters only ever see their own jobs and the applications to them. Whether they can open CVs of candidates who did not apply depends on your talent pool settings. See CV privacy and talent access.

Recruiters on a Business plan board can also manage their jobs and applications by chatting with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, through the AI assistant for recruiters.
