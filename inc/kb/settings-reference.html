Open Modern Job Board → Settings. The settings are grouped into four sections in the side menu. Each section has its own Save button.

Job board

Listings

Job Posting Rules

Who can post jobs: Recruiters only (the default), Administrators only, or Anyone who is logged in.

Search Filters

Turn each filter on the jobs page on or off: Keywords, Location, Category, Type and Company.

Board Mode

Branding

Text overrides

Change any wording the plugin shows without editing code. Select Add replacement, type the text exactly as the plugin writes it (for example Find Jobs) and what to show instead, then save. A replacement applies everywhere that text appears; translations still work for everything you don't override.

People & access

Registration

Recruiter approval and Candidate approval hold new accounts as pending until a staff member approves them. The person is told their account is waiting.

Application Security

CV Privacy & Talent

See CV privacy and talent access.

Collaborators

Invite a teammate by email to give them the Board Collaborator role instead of making them a WordPress administrator. Someone who already has an account gets the role straight away; anyone else gets a link to create an account, valid for 7 days. The section lists your current collaborators.

Plans & payments

  • License & Plan: your license key and current plan. See Plans and license keys.
  • Payments (Pro): an overview of members with job credits, CV access, paid alert slots and trials, with links to WooCommerce, recent board orders and how to set up package products; then Require Payment for new jobs, the WooCommerce products for job submission and CV unlocks, and Paid CV Access.
  • Memberships & Trials (Pro): Trial job credits and trial CV access days for new recruiters, and the number of free job alert slots per candidate.

See Charging employers with WooCommerce.

Integrations

  • Google Jobs & Webhooks: the Google Jobs type map and static fields (see SEO and Google for Jobs), and webhook URLs with their secret (Business) (see Webhooks).
  • Job Ingestion & Backfill: the secret for the job ingest route (Business), and what to show when a search finds nothing. See Job ingest.
  • Email: sending through SMTP, branded email layout, and Mailchimp. See Email notifications and templates.
  • Social Auto-Post: a webhook address that receives each newly published job, for posting to social channels through Zapier, Make or n8n.
  • API Keys: create and revoke keys for systems that call the public REST API. A new key is shown once, with a Copy button. See REST API authentication.
  • Embed widget: a line of code that shows your latest jobs on another website, with a Copy button and a preview.
