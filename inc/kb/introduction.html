Modern Job Board turns a WordPress site into a complete job board. Employers post and manage jobs from the front end, candidates search, apply, save jobs and set up alerts, and you run everything from one admin console inside WordPress.

It is a self-hosted plugin, not a hosted service. Your jobs, companies, applications and accounts live in your own WordPress database, and CVs are stored in a private folder on your own server. Nothing is sent to us.

What you get

  • A searchable job board with predictive search on title, company and location, filter chips, and readable URLs such as /jobs/in/remote/ that can be shared and indexed.
  • Recruiter and candidate accounts with multi-step signup and their own dashboards for jobs, applications, profiles, saved jobs and alerts.
  • Private CV storage: CVs are kept outside the Media Library and are only sent to signed-in users who are allowed to see them.
  • An admin console with a board health dashboard, a jobs console with bulk actions and CSV export, and settings grouped by task.
  • SEO for jobs: JobPosting structured data on every job page, so listings are eligible for Google for Jobs.
  • Blocks and shortcodes for every front-end page, so the board fits into any theme.
