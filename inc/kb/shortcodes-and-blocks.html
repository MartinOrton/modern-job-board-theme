Blocks

Eight blocks cover the core pages. You find them under Modern Job Board in the block inserter. Each block does exactly what the matching shortcode does, so you can use either.

Jobs on your blog

Career guides and articles can send readers to matching jobs:

  • Give your blog categories the same slugs as your job categories, for example engineering.
  • A blog post whose first category matches a job category gets a Related Jobs list of up to five open jobs at the end, automatically.
  • To place the list yourself, or to pick another category, add [mjb_related_jobs category="engineering"] to the post.
  • Link from your jobs pages to the blog as well, so search engines find both.

To show a shortcode as text on a page, for example in your own documentation, write it with double brackets: [[mjb_jobs]] is displayed as [mjb_jobs] and is not run.

Careers sites

When the board runs as a single-company careers site, the job form, recruiter dashboard, talent pool, packages and featured companies are switched off for visitors, even if their shortcodes are on a page. See Companies and careers mode.
